Hey Vox - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Hey Vox is a voice assistant that runs entirely in your web browser. It only knows what you choose to connect.

What it accesses

If you press "Connect Google Calendar" or "Connect Gmail" and sign in with Google, Hey Vox asks for read-only access: your upcoming calendar events, or the subject, sender, date and preview of your latest inbox emails. It cannot send, edit or delete anything.

What happens to your data

Your data is loaded into the open page in your browser and used only to answer your questions. It is not sent to any server we operate, not stored, not shared, and not used for advertising or to train any model. When you close or reload the page, it is gone. "Clear all data" removes it immediately.

Voice

Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition, which is provided by your browser vendor under its own terms.

Revoking access

You can remove Hey Vox's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
