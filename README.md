# Hey Vox

A voice assistant that only knows what you connect.

- Say "Hey Vox" (the wake name is changeable) or press the orb, and talk.
- Connect your own data: a calendar file (.ics), notes, CSV or pasted text. Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
- Ask what is on your calendar, search or summarize your notes, list open commitments, or run a spoken debrief that ends in a Promise Receipt.
- It says so honestly when something is not connected.

Voice input needs Chrome or Edge and microphone permission. Typing always works.

It is rule-based and runs entirely on your device: no accounts, no tracking, no network calls.
